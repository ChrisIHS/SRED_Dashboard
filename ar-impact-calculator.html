Methodology: Total AR hours = staff × % time on AR × monthly working hours. Carrying cost = sales/month × (annual rate ÷ 12), representing the monthly cost of capital tied up in receivables. Blended cost/hr = (staff cost + carrying cost) ÷ AR hours. Working capital cost per hour of delay = total monthly sales ÷ AR staff hours. Working capital added = sales/month × time-reduction %, reflecting capital released by a faster cycle — counted once, not compounded monthly. Annualized benefit = working capital added (one time) + 12 months of staff cost savings + 12 months of carrying cost savings. Equivalent sales ($) to match monthly Staff cost savings = monthly staff cost savings ÷ average profit margin %, i.e. the sales volume needed to generate the same dollar impact through margin alone. Annual sales required to cover annual staff savings = 12 months of staff cost savings ÷ average profit margin %. Working Capital ROI = working capital freed ÷ annualized EMI cost (monthly EMI investment × 12). Annualized Benefit ROI = annualized benefit ÷ annualized EMI cost. Figures are illustrative estimates for discussion purposes and should be validated against the client's actual AR data.
